Account and profile

Everything about your access to SyntaxQuest.

How do I create an account? ▾

You can sign up with email/password, Google or Apple Sign-In. You can also start as a guest and create an account later without losing your progress.

How do I change my username? ▾

Go to Profile → Edit and pick a unique username. Availability is validated in real time. You can change it once every 30 days.

I forgot my password ▾

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot your password?". We will email you a reset link. If you signed up with Google/Apple, sign in with that same provider.

How do I delete my account? ▾

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This action is irreversible and erases your progress, gems and active subscriptions. You can also request deletion by writing to us.

Is my progress saved in the cloud? ▾

Yes, as long as you are signed in. Your progress syncs automatically with Firestore so you can continue on another device. In guest mode data stays on the device only.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium, Pro Max, gems and refunds.

How does the 7-day trial work? ▾

Eligible plans may show a 7-day free trial from the App Store the first time you activate them. Availability is confirmed by Apple during purchase. You can cancel from iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How do I cancel my subscription? ▾

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SyntaxQuest → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

Can I request a refund? ▾

Refunds are handled by Apple, not directly by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and select the purchase. If Apple approves it, we will automatically move your plan back to Free.

I bought gems but they are not showing up ▾

Wait 1–2 minutes and restart the app. If they still do not appear, go to Profile → Restore purchases. If it keeps failing, contact us with the transaction ID (you will find it in Apple's receipt email).

What is the difference between Premium and Pro Max? ▾

Premium unlocks selected advanced courses and features, unlimited lives, an ad-free experience and monthly Guided Learning quotas. Pro Max includes the entire catalog, a larger AI quota, guided free-topic mode and visual perks inside the app. Current prices and trials are shown in the App Store before you confirm.

How do I upgrade from Premium to Pro Max? ▾

Open the paywall in the app and select Pro Max. Apple handles the plan change and any applicable billing adjustment.

Can I restore purchases on another device? ▾

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID, open SyntaxQuest and go to Profile → Restore purchases. Your subscription and gems will be reactivated.

Technical issues

Bugs, syncing, offline and compatibility.

The app crashes unexpectedly ▾

Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store, restart your iPhone and free up space if you have less than 1 GB. If it persists, write to us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

My progress is not syncing ▾

Check that you have an internet connection and that you are signed in (not in guest mode). Go to Settings → Sync now. If you use offline mode, progress will upload once you are back online.

Code will not run in the Playground ▾

JavaScript, SQL, HTML/CSS and Python run locally on your device. Java, Swift, Kotlin, C++ and C# require a connection (they use Judge0 CE in the cloud). If it fails, check your connection.

Does it work on iPad and Apple Watch? ▾

Yes. SyntaxQuest works on iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad and has an Apple Watch companion with streak reminders. It also includes home screen widgets.

How do I download courses for offline use? ▾

Courses bundled in the app can be marked as available offline. If you complete actions without a connection, SyntaxQuest queues them locally and tries to sync when you are back online.
